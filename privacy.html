H.A.K.I. Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

H.A.K.I. (Highlighting All Key Interactions) is an unofficial fan tool for the One Piece Card Game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandai, Toei Animation or Shueisha.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send any data about you or how you use it to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Decks you build. Saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Card images. Cached on your phone after they are downloaded.
  • Preview cards. If you check for previews (Premium), the list is saved on your phone until you remove it.
  • Deck images you save. Written to your phone's Downloads folder. On Android 9 and older this asks for storage permission, used only for that.
  • Your settings. Whether Premium is unlocked, and the colour theme you picked.
  • A crash report. If the app crashes, the error is saved on your phone so it can be shown to you on the next launch. It is never sent anywhere.

None of this is backed up or shared. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of it.

Network use

All card data ships inside the app and works offline. Apart from Google Play's purchase check (below), the app makes two kinds of network request:

  • Card images, loaded over HTTPS from Bandai's official card site (en.onepiece-cardgame.com). Like any website, that server receives your IP address and the image requested. Bandai's own privacy policy covers what they do with it.
  • Preview cards, only when you tap "Check for previews". The app downloads one file over HTTPS from hakiappdev.github.io, a site hosted by GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address, as with any website, under GitHub's privacy statement. The app sends nothing about you with the request.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The app only asks Google Play whether Premium is owned. The developer sees Google's standard order record for a purchase, never your payment details.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
